# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve this teaching framework.

## Ground rules

1. **Teach, do not sell.** No product pitches, affiliate links, or vendor endorsements.
2. **No invented statistics.** Cite a primary source for any figure, or omit it.
3. **Stay original.** Do not paste or lightly rewrite third-party frameworks, ISAC papers, or employer materials.
4. **Respect safety.** No exploit payloads, production abuse recipes, or guidance that ignores life-safety and patient-safety limits.
5. **Plain language.** Avoid em dashes and filler words that add heat without meaning.
6. **License.** Contributions are accepted under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE). By submitting, you agree your contribution may be released under that license with attribution to the project.

## How to propose a change

Open an issue describing the gap. Keep pull requests focused. Update the matching sector overlay for sector-specific changes; update `FRAMEWORK.md` for sector-neutral changes. Mark example scores as format examples, not claims about a named organization.

## Scope of this repo

In scope: clarity of the cycle, honesty of metrics, sector safety constraints, backlog quality, executive reporting.

Out of scope: tooling integrations, SaaS products, private client playbooks, and materials that name employers or closed communities as authorities for this text.

## Security testing ethics

Discuss techniques only at the level needed to design detections and safety gates. Authorization comes first. Contributions that primarily help attack systems the reader does not own will be rejected.
